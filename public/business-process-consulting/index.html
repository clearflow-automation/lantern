BEFORE WE TALK

Questions owners ask.

Do you need to know our industry already? +

Your team knows the work. We learn by following recent jobs and checking the resulting map with the people doing them. Where a trade already has a suitable tool, we configure that tool before building a substitute.

Will you write SOPs for the whole company? +

No. We begin with one recurring process. A whole-company promise hides the real scope and usually produces documents nobody uses.

Do we need new software? +

Not by default. Existing tools come first. Any new tool, subscription or implementation cost is discussed before it enters scope.

Can AI automate the process? +

Possibly, but only after the process, inputs, approval rules and human review are clear. Automation is a possible tool, not the promised outcome.

What happens after the pilot? +

We review the evidence together. You can stop with the completed work, improve another process or commission a clearly defined additional piece.
