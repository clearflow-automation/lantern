01 / TERMS

What These Terms Cover

These terms cover Lantern’s website, work examples and contact links. They explain what you can expect when browsing the site. Client engagements have a separate agreed scope and terms.

02 / TERMS

What The Examples Represent

Each example retains its stated status: an internal tool, personal build, experiment, reference workflow, client website or sample design. A working build demonstrates what was made; it does not establish results for another business.

Department example videos are labelled placeholders. Admin & Operations and Orchestrating Agents currently show illustrative workflows rather than completed builds. The HR example was built from the applicant’s side; repurposing it for a recruitment team requires an adapted workflow.

03 / TERMS

How To Use The Information

Use the examples to explore what a tool or process could look like for your business. They do not guarantee a saving, business result, lead volume or automation outcome.

Finance examples show market research, pricing models and document-review workflows. They do not supply investment recommendations or final tax-filing decisions. The relevant person or professional remains responsible for reviewing and approving their work.

04 / TERMS

Calls And Client Engagements

The first conversation is free and exploratory. Booking a call or sending an email does not create a client engagement or commit you to paid work.

Before an engagement starts, we agree its scope, responsibilities, fees, timings, data access and acceptance terms separately. Client ownership and handover arrangements belong in that agreement. If it differs from these website terms, the agreed client document governs the engagement.

05 / TERMS

Using Website Material

You can read the site and share links to its public pages. Ask for written permission before republishing, selling or presenting Lantern’s original copy, diagrams or downloadable material as your own work.

Client and third-party material remains subject to its owner’s rights.

06 / TERMS

External Links And Availability

Booking, portfolio and project links may take you to another website. Those providers control their own services, availability and terms.

We aim to keep Lantern’s website accurate and available. Pages and examples may be updated, removed or temporarily unavailable.
