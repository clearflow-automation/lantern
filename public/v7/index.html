Questions shop owners actually ask

₹9,999 mein poori website? Kuch gadbad toh nahi?
No catch. At ₹9,999 you get one page, built properly, live in two days, with the call and WhatsApp buttons that actually bring you work. What it does not include is the Google setup and your own domain — that is ₹15,000, and it is the difference between having a website and being found.
Har mahine paisa dena padega?
No. Not to us, ever. Hosting and the security certificate cost us nearly nothing, so we don't bill you for them. The only recurring cost is your domain, roughly ₹1,000 a year, paid directly to the registrar in your own name.
Site kiski hogi agar main chala jaun?
Yours. The domain is registered in your name on your email from the first day. We configure it; we never hold it. If you leave, you get the files and the domain moves with you. There is nothing to negotiate because there is nothing of yours that we're holding.
Photos achhi nahi hain mere paas.
Most shops don't have them, and it's the single most common reason a site never gets finished. Your phone is enough. I'll tell you exactly which five shots to take and where to stand. If there's genuinely nothing usable, we design around it rather than filling the page with stock photos of someone else's shop.
Aap gayab toh nahi ho jaoge?
Fair question to ask a one-person studio, and the honest answer is that you are holding the safeguard: the domain is yours, the hosting account can be yours, and the site is a plain set of files you can hand to anyone. Nothing here depends on me still being around.
Will I actually come up on Google?
On Complete and above you'll be set up correctly for it: the right page title, your address and hours marked up the way Google reads them, a sitemap submitted to Search Console. That last step is the one almost nobody does. What nobody honest can promise you is a position — anyone guaranteeing you the top spot is selling you something else.
