Questions shop owners actually ask

₹4,999 mein poori website? Kuch gadbad toh nahi?

No catch, but there is a limit, and it's a visible one. At ₹4,999 you write the text yourself and the site sits on a Lantern web address rather than your own domain. Everything that makes it work (the speed, the buttons, the Google setup) is the same. The ₹9,999 one is where you get your own domain and I do the writing.

Do I have to pay something every month?

No. Not to us, ever. Hosting and the security certificate cost us nearly nothing, so we don't bill you for them. The only recurring cost is your domain, roughly ₹1,000 a year, paid directly to the registrar in your own name.

Who owns the site if I stop working with you?

You do. The domain is registered in your name on your email from the first day. We configure it; we never hold it. If you leave, you get the files and the domain moves with you. There is nothing to negotiate because there is nothing of yours that we're holding.

I don't have good photos.

Most shops don't, and it's the single most common reason a site never gets finished. Your phone is enough. I'll tell you exactly which five shots to take and where to stand. If there's genuinely nothing usable, we design around it rather than filling the page with stock photos of someone else's shop.

Will I actually come up on Google?

You'll be set up correctly for it: the right page title, your address and hours marked up the way Google reads them, a sitemap submitted to Search Console. That's the part almost nobody does, and it's why most new sites never appear at all. What nobody honest can promise you is a position. Anyone guaranteeing you the top spot is selling you something else.

How do I know you won't disappear?

Fair question to ask a one-person studio, and the honest answer is that you're holding the safeguard: the domain is yours, the hosting account can be yours, and the site is a plain set of files you can hand to anyone. Nothing here depends on me still being around.
