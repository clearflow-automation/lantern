Data Privacy & Deployment Details +

Hosting, access, ownership and handover are agreed before building. Keeping data on your premises requires a verified local setup, including integrations, logs and backups.

For AI-assisted tasks, we minimise the data used and assess whether redaction or anonymisation is suitable. Removing names alone may leave people identifiable. AI outputs still need checks.

Applicable GDPR and DPDP requirements need assessment for each implementation. AI-assisted development, local hosting or anonymisation alone does not establish compliance.
