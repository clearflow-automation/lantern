BEFORE WE TALK

Questions
owners ask.

Have something else in mind?

Do you know our industry? +

Your team knows it. We learn how your business works by speaking with the people doing the work, observing real jobs and checking the resulting map with them. The process is adapted to your business.

What do you need from us? +

A named person, a few hours a week and access to the relevant records in your own systems. We agree the people, time and scope before starting. A written note goes out every Friday, with a thirty-minute call every two weeks.

Do we have to buy new software? +

We start with what you already use and what your trade already sells. An existing tracker, configured properly, comes before a custom build. Any new tool and its cost are discussed first.

Who owns what you make? +

You do: the map, checklists, templates, numbers and operating record. If the engagement ends, you keep the work.

What if we cannot measure something? +

We mark it as unmeasured beside the relevant figure. The report distinguishes measured costs, estimates and open questions.

What happens after eight weeks? +

We review the pilot together. You can stop with the work completed, address another process, or commission a specific piece. Continued work is a separate decision.

What stays outside the engagement? +

We document and improve how work moves. Your team continues to operate the business.

  • Your professionals retain filings, submissions, approvals and professional advice.
  • Credentials remain with your team; client records stay in your own systems.
  • AI-generated work is a draft for your team to review.
  • We do not promise savings, margin increases or headcount reductions.
  • We charge for the service, not a share of a regulated professional’s fees.
