No. We read, write down, and build alongside. Nothing live is ours to operate.
Who owns what you make?
You. The map, the checklists, the numbers, the templates. If we stopped tomorrow you would keep a documented business.
What if a number is wrong?
Anything we couldn't measure is marked as unmeasured next to the figure. A gap is honest. A confident wrong number is not.
How long does it take?
Eight weeks for the first process, spread across your calendar, and about five working days of our time for each one after. Your season wins.

Almost never. Whatever your trade already uses is set up first. Anything new has to prove a sheet can't do it.
