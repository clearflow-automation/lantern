Privacy Notice.

Reviewed: 4 October 2026

How we handle website visits, enquiries and appointment information.

ON THIS PAGE

01 / PRIVACY

Who Handles Your Information

Lantern is operated by Nandit Vaish from Gurugram, India. For privacy questions or requests, email vaishnandit@airlantern.com.

02 / PRIVACY

Information We Receive

If you email Lantern or book a call, we receive the contact details and business information you choose to provide. Booking details can include your name, email address and chosen appointment time.

The public website’s measurement services receive visit information such as pages viewed, device category, referral source and approximate location. Campaign labels in a link can identify which message led to a visit.

03 / PRIVACY

How We Use It

We use enquiry and appointment information to respond, arrange your conversation and understand what you need. If an enquiry becomes an engagement, relevant information forms part of the agreed work and necessary business records.

Website measurement helps us understand which pages and messages are useful. Technical information also supports site delivery, security and troubleshooting.

04 / PRIVACY

Services Connected To This Site

  • Website delivery: Cloudflare provides hosting and security. It processes technical request information, which can include an IP address.
  • Website measurement: the existing public-site setup includes Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics. Google Analytics can use cookies. These services process visit information under their own privacy terms.
  • Fonts: these pages serve fonts locally. Older website portfolio pages request Google Fonts; Google receives the technical information needed to deliver those fonts, including an IP address.
  • Booking and email: booking links open Google Calendar. Email links open your chosen email application. The information you submit through those services reaches Lantern as part of your enquiry or booking.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy ↗ · Cloudflare Privacy Policy ↗ · Google Fonts privacy ↗

05 / PRIVACY

Sharing And Keeping Records

We use the service providers needed to deliver the site, arrange conversations and carry out agreed work. Information may also be disclosed where the law requires it.

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and manage the resulting relationship. Engagement, contractual and other required records may need to be kept longer. This notice does not set a single retention period for every type of record.

06 / PRIVACY

Your Choices And Requests

You can choose how much information to include in an initial enquiry and use browser controls to block or remove cookies. Google and Cloudflare also provide information about their own privacy controls.

You can ask Lantern to explain, correct or delete the enquiry information it holds by emailing vaishnandit@airlantern.com. We may ask for enough information to verify the request and will explain if a record needs to be retained.

07 / PRIVACY

Data Used During Client Work

Access to business records, use of AI services, data handling and retention are agreed for each engagement. This website notice does not replace those arrangements. A workflow’s data location depends on its design and providers; using AI or fixed rules alone does not guarantee that data stays on your premises.

08 / PRIVACY

Contact And Updates

Questions about this notice can be sent to vaishnandit@airlantern.com. We update the notice when the website’s practices change and show the review date above.
