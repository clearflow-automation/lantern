WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information airlantern.com processes when someone visits the website, emails Lantern or books a conversation.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Who operates this website

Lantern is operated by Nandit Vaish from Gurugram, India. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to vaishnandit@airlantern.com.

Information the website receives

  • Basic technical information about a visit, such as pages viewed, device category, approximate location and referral source.
  • Campaign labels included in a link, such as UTM source, campaign and creative names.
  • Information a person chooses to provide by email or while booking an appointment.

How the information is used

  • To respond to an enquiry or prepare for a requested conversation.
  • To understand which pages and campaign messages are useful.
  • To maintain website security and diagnose technical problems.
  • To keep necessary business records where an enquiry becomes an engagement.

Measurement and external services

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics. These services may process technical and usage information under their own privacy terms. Appointment links open Google Calendar, and email links open the visitor’s chosen email service. Lantern does not receive appointment or email details until the visitor chooses to submit them through that service.

Advertising links

A paid advertisement may add campaign labels to the page URL so Lantern can understand which message led to a visit or booking-link click. A click is not treated as a completed booking or qualified business enquiry. If additional advertising technology is introduced, this notice will be updated before it is used.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to host, measure or respond to the interaction, or where disclosure is required by law. Enquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the conversation, the resulting engagement, record-keeping or dispute resolution.

Your choices

Visitors can block analytics through browser or device controls. A person can ask Lantern to correct or delete enquiry information by emailing the address above. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business, contractual or legal reasons.

This is a plain-language description of Lantern’s current website practices. It is not a promise that third-party services will never change their own processing.
